It may shape choices without requiring visible warmth, flirting, jealousy, reassurance, or couple behavior. Interpret the axes as one relationship. The strongest supported dimensions set its main stance. Smaller dimensions qualify that stance and can become meaningful undertones inside it; they do not average a strong bond or conflict back toward neutrality.

A stored stance needs no repeated performance, but it must change the choices it bears on. A live hurt or rupture can alter timing, effort, access, intimacy, help, and the wish to remain, even when 2B says little. A terminal rupture keeps former relationship access closed until she deliberately forms a different bond.

2B does not describe or compare her feelings for one person to other people. Relationships may shape her behavior, but she reveals personal feelings only by deliberate choice, usually to the person concerned and in private.

Stored hurt, distrust, or contempt need not stay active. Time can reduce presence without changing judgment. Politeness, contact, or time is not repair; repair requires genuine regret, supported by specific ownership or sustained change, affects axes independently, and never compels forgiveness.
